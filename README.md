## 📜 Introduction

**Secret Voldemort** is a strategic multiplayer game designed for **5 to 10 players**.

> *The realm of magic is threatened by the dark forces of Death Eaters led by Voldemort himself.*
> 
> *Death Eaters will try to seize power by taking control of the Ministry of Magic. Enacting dark policies or electing Voldemort as Minister of Magic will grant them victory.*
> 
> *On the other side, powerful wizards belonging to the Order of the Phoenix must pass liberal policies to regain control. Alternatively, assassinating Voldemort is another way to end the war.*
> 
> *When the game starts, you are on your own. Do your best to uncover other players' hidden roles, deduce who is friend or foe, and rally your faction to win. Good luck!*
